4. Location

Location helps people find talent and castings near them. When you create an account, a post or an AI model, the app asks for permission to use your device’s location (“While Using the App” only — we never track you in the background).

  • Your device turns the coordinates into a city and country using the phone’s built-in location services.
  • We store your city, region, country and approximate coordinates with your profile, and the city, country and coordinates with each post and AI model.
  • Other users only ever see your city and country, plus an approximate distance such as “12 km away”. Your exact coordinates are never shown.
  • You can decline location access and type your city and country instead, edit them at any time, or turn location off in your device settings.

5. AI features and your consent

When you create an account you consent to AI Modeling Studio’s AI features, including AI models, AI-generated content, AI suggestions and AI-assisted messaging.

  • When you use the AI assistant, the text and context needed for your request are sent to our AI service provider to generate a reply. This happens only to deliver the feature you asked for.
  • Our AI providers process this data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it to train their own models for other customers or to sell it.
  • AI output can be wrong. Review anything the AI drafts before you post or send it.
  • AI models and AI posts are labelled “AI” across the app so people always know what is real.
